Bankruptcy Navigator

Guidance and resources for navigating the bankruptcy process

Bankruptcy Navigator is a resource designed to help individuals and businesses understand their options when facing financial hardship. Whether you are exploring Chapter 7, Chapter 11, or Chapter 13 bankruptcy, this site provides clear, accessible information to help you make informed decisions.

Chapter 7 — Liquidation

A fresh start for individuals. Non-exempt assets may be sold to repay creditors, and most remaining unsecured debts are discharged at the end of the process.

Chapter 11 — Reorganization

Primarily for businesses. Allows a debtor to remain operational while restructuring debts under a court-approved repayment plan.

Chapter 13 — Repayment Plan

For individuals with regular income. Debts are reorganized into a 3–5 year repayment plan, allowing debtors to keep their assets.

Automatic Stay

Filing for bankruptcy triggers an automatic stay that immediately halts most collection actions, foreclosures, repossessions, and wage garnishments.

Means Test

To qualify for Chapter 7, your income must fall below a threshold or pass a means test comparing income to allowable expenses.

Exemptions

Federal and state exemptions protect certain property (home equity, vehicle, retirement accounts) from liquidation during bankruptcy.
